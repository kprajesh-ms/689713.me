exHubNetScan Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

exHubNetScan is a network scanner for macOS and Windows made by Rajesh Pillai (exHub). This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: everything stays on your computer, and we collect nothing.

What the app does on your network

When you run a scan, exHubNetScan sends standard discovery traffic (ping, ARP, Bonjour/mDNS, SSDP, NetBIOS, TCP connection attempts) to devices on the local subnet you chose, and shows what it learns: IP and MAC addresses, vendor names, hostnames, open ports and device types. This information is stored only on your computer — in the app's own data folder and in any scan files you choose to save — and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Names, types and notes you add to devices are stored locally, keyed by the device's MAC address.

Internet connections the app makes

The app contacts the internet only for these features, and only when you use them:

  • Speed test and public-IP lookup (Internet page): test data is exchanged with Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com), and the public IP, ISP and approximate location are read from Cloudflare's metadata endpoint or, if that is unavailable, from ipinfo.io. Those services see your public IP address like any website you visit; exHubNetScan does not store or transmit the result anywhere.
  • Update check (direct-download builds only): the app fetches a small version file from 689713.me. The App Store and Microsoft Store builds do not do this.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Microsoft (Microsoft Store) under their privacy policies. Direct-purchase licence keys are verified offline on your computer.

The app has no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising, and no account system.

Bug reports

If you choose Report a bug, the app writes a JSON file containing its event log, the last scan result and basic system information to a location you pick, and opens an email draft addressed to kprajesh@me.com. Nothing is sent unless you attach the file and send the email yourself. You can open the file and remove anything before sending it.

Data we hold

If you email us, we keep the email to answer it. That is the only personal data we hold.

Children

exHubNetScan is a general-purpose utility rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's behaviour changes. The effective date at the top tells you when it was last revised.
